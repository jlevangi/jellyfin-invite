Install Seerr on your phone

Open Seerr and sign in with your Keycloak account.

Open Seerr{{ requests_url }}

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Seerr in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. If the option is missing, use Safari and scroll the Share list.

Android

  1. Open Seerr in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm the prompt. The browser may use different wording.

Request something

  1. Search for a movie or show.
  2. Open a result and select Request.
  3. Pending means processing; Approved means accepted; Available means ready in Jellyfin. Popular releases may take about 30 minutes; niche items can take a day or two.

Watching on the Web Mobile and TV Setup

Using the Website

Keycloak sign-in works only in a web browser. Use Quick Connect for the Jellyfin mobile app or TV, and approve it from Jellyfin in your signed-in browser.
  1. Open {{ jellyfin_url }} on a phone or computer.
  2. Choose Sign in with Keycloak—do not type into the ordinary Jellyfin password boxes.

Set up Mobile or TV (Quick Connect)

  1. In your phone or computer's browser, open Jellyfin and choose Sign in with Keycloak. Stay signed in.
  2. Install and open the Jellyfin app on your device. Enter the server address below.

    Tap the address to copy it into the Jellyfin app:

  3. Choose Quick Connect on the app's login screen (sometimes under Manual Login). Keep the displayed code open.
  4. Switch back to Jellyfin in your browser. Open your profile menu, then Settings → Quick Connect.
  5. Enter the code from your device and confirm. Your device will sign in automatically.
Note: If playback is slow, lower playback quality in player settings. If you get stuck, tell Pierce your device/browser, step, and any error screenshot.
