Install Seerr on your phone

Open Seerr and sign in with your Keycloak account.

Tap the address to copy it:

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open Seerr in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. If the option is missing, use Safari and scroll the Share list.

Android

  1. Open Seerr in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm the prompt. The browser may use different wording.

Seerr is a separate site, so this guide cannot detect whether installation finished. Complete it there, or skip and return here.

Request something

  1. Search for a movie or show.
  2. Open a result and select Request.
  3. Pending means processing; Approved means accepted; Available means ready in Jellyfin. Popular releases may take about 30 minutes; niche items can take a day or two.

Set up your TV with Quick Connect

  1. Enter the server address above.
  2. Choose Quick Connect on your TV.
  3. Find the code displayed on your TV.
  4. Enter the code on your signed-in phone.
Note: If playback is slow, lower playback quality in player settings. If you get stuck, tell Pierce your device/browser, step, and any error screenshot.
